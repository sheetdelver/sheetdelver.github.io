# Image Credits

Product screenshots in `assets/shots/` and the social card
`assets/og-card.jpg` are captures of SheetDelver itself, taken with
disposable test worlds (provenance in `assets/shots/MANIFEST.md`). The only
third-party artwork on the site is what those test worlds display inside the
screenshots, listed below.
